Welcome to the Sugar Land Student Center

The Sugar Land Student Center is a hub for students to connect, learn, and grow. Our center offers a variety of resources and programs to support student success.

Conference Room and Reservation Services

The CARS team can assist registered student organizations, campus departments, and off-campus individuals in hosting meetings, conferences, retreats, and other special events within the Student Centers.

Game Room

The Game Room is a recreational space for students to relax and have fun. It features a variety of games and activities to enjoy with friends.

Creation Station

The Creation Station is a creative space where students can engage in arts and crafts, DIY projects, and other hands-on activities.

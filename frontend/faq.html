Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to see the answer.

What are the Student Center hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and Saturday to Sunday from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Who can book a room?

Registered student organizations, campus departments, and off-campus guests can all book a room.

How do I book a room?

Fill out the form on the Book a Room page. Our team will email you to confirm your booking.

What can I do in the Game Room?

You can play billiards, table tennis, and video games with your friends. Bring your Cougar Card to check out equipment.

How do I contact the Student Center?

Email us at scinfo@central.uh.edu or call 832-842-4113.
